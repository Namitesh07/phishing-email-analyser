Protocol 01: triage safely

3 steps

A risk score tells you to pause. This protocol helps you decide what to do without giving an attacker more information.

  1. 1
    Pause

    Do not click a link, open an attachment, or reply in a hurry.

  2. 2
    Verify independently

    Use the organisation’s official app, website, or a contact method you already trust.

  3. 3
    Report and preserve

    Keep the original message and use your email provider or organisation’s phishing-report option.

Read the score in context

0–19Low risk
20–59Suspicious
60–100High risk

A low score does not prove that a message is genuine. Context and independent verification still matter.

! Never share passwords, recovery codes, or MFA/OTP codes by email.

Method notes

Nothing is hidden behind the score.

Each rule has a fixed point value. A score of 100 means the combined signals reached the cap; it does not mean the email is definitively malicious.
